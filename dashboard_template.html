    What's on each card

    The big number is the model's chance the picked team wins. The bar under it shows the same thing.

    With odds connected, each card shows the best price across sportsbooks and its value (EV): green is +5% or better, yellow is 0 to +5%, red means the price is too short.

    Spread leans come from how the model's past disagreements with Vegas actually played out, so a lean can go against the projected winner.
